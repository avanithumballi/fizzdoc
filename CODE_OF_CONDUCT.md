# Code of Conduct

Fizzdoc follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be kind, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the people who use Fizzdoc. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, [Rishab Dugar](https://github.com/kingrishabdugar), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break these rules.
